About BZARLU

BZARLU is pure studio creation—no stages, no DJ booths, no crowds.
Just me in the room, turning real emotions into rolling subs, liquid melodies, and drops that hit different.

I write my own lyrics from personal stories, start beats with Suno Pro foundations, then shape everything until it feels right. Tracks like "The Dream" are quiet confessions set to bass; "I am gonna" is the weekend escape when you need to forget.

No live shows, no tours—just music made for headphones, late nights, and playlists. Independent, digital-first, all feels.
If a drop ever made you pause and remember something you tried to forget… that's the point.
